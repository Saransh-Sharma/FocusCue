Privacy & Security

Local-first by default.

  • Script content is stored locally on your Mac.
  • Voice-aware features process audio locally by default.
  • Audio recordings are not stored by this marketing site.
  • Cloud speech and AI features are optional and only used if you enable and configure them.
  • Analytics are off by default.
