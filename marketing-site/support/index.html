  • External display and mirror output troubleshooting
  • App Store launch timing and availability questions

Self-help quick checks

  • Verify macOS microphone and speech recognition permissions in Privacy & Security settings.
  • Make sure both devices are on the same network when using browser remote output.
  • Use Classic mode as a fallback if speech-based modes are unavailable during setup.

Before you email support

Include your macOS version, the FocusCue feature you were using, and whether the issue happened in Word Tracking, Voice-Activated, or Classic mode. That makes troubleshooting much faster.
